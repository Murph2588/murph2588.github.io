What I do

I lead customer success and platform implementation at TrustArc, where enterprise teams come to stand up privacy and compliance programs — data mapping, DSARs, risk assessments — and actually get them adopted, not just installed. I own implementation and ongoing success for a book of 50+ enterprise accounts, and most of what I do comes down to one question: does this make someone's job easier, or did we just add another login?

A good part of my work now is building the systems my own team runs on. I created TrustArc's Ask Arc Prompt Library — an eleven-section, product-by-product guide now used company-wide across Customer Success, Sales, and Marketing — and I co-host a webinar series on getting real value out of AI in privacy work. I've also led some of the harder migrations: a 200-plus-request DSAR migration off a competitor's platform, and a cross-region data migration finished in half an hour instead of weeks.

Ship the capability, and most people find their own way eventually. Ship the know-how alongside it, and adoption stops being a project.

I try to take my own advice here: this year I've been going deep on agentic AI tools myself — Claude Code, multi-agent workflows, actually building with the things I tell customers to adopt — because it's hard to coach someone through a shift you haven't gone through yourself.
